Security, identity and compliance principles

Track G. Separate authentication from authorisation; learn OAuth 2.0, OIDC, JWT and PKCE with a validation checklist; take identity only from the verified token and check every object access; keep secrets out of code and logs; use keyless, short-lived service identity; scan delivery and uploads; cap abuse per cost vector; speak control and privacy vocabulary.
